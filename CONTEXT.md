# nathandoherty.dev

The portfolio site of Nathan Doherty: projects, blog posts and employment history, published as a static site.

## Language

**Published**:
A project or blog post that is not hidden and whose publish date has arrived. Only published entries are listed on the site.
_Avoid_: Live, visible

**Scheduled**:
A project or blog post that is not hidden and whose publish date has not arrived, or a role whose start date has not arrived. Its page is reachable by direct link, but it is not listed anywhere on the site and is kept out of search engines.
_Avoid_: Draft, future-dated, unpublished

**Hidden**:
A project or blog post that is not ready for anyone to see. It has no page and is not listed.
_Avoid_: Draft, unlisted

**Latest activity**:
The later of an entry's publish date and last-modified date. Published entries are always ordered by it, most recent first.
_Avoid_: Most recent, last updated

**Latest role**:
The employment entry with the most recent start date that has already arrived, whether or not it has ended.
_Avoid_: Current role, current employment
